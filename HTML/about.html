About JS FRESHCUTS

JS FRESHCUTS is a barbershop that started in 2023 to provide quality and affordable haircuts to UFS students.

The business was started because there are not many barbershops around the UFS area. JS FRESHCUTS makes it easier for students to get a haircut close to where they live.

The business is growing from the ground up. Although it started with limited equipment, the barber has strong barbering skills and is focused on growing the business.

Our Goal

The goal of JS FRESHCUTS is to become a well-known barber in Bloemfontein and eventually become known throughout South Africa.
